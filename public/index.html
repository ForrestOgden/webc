YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Random video chat, made simple.

Roam is a browser-based way to meet new people in one-to-one video conversations. Your matches depend on who is actually online; no prerecorded feeds or fake participants.

Is Roam random video chat free?

Yes. Roam is free to use in your browser. You do not need to create an account to start searching for an available partner.

How do I start random video chat?

Select your region and preferred match region, confirm you are at least 18, and choose Start video chatting. Allow camera and microphone access when your browser requests it. Roam searches for another available, compatible participant.

Can I skip to another person?

Yes. Select Next person to leave the current chat and search for someone else. You can end a session or turn off your microphone and camera at any time.

Is Roam available on phones?

Roam is designed for modern mobile and desktop browsers that support WebRTC video calls. Camera and microphone permissions are required to join.

Roam is for adults 18+ only. Be careful with personal information and use community rules, Block, and Report to help keep conversations safer. You can also review the privacy information before joining.
