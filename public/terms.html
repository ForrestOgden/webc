Terms of use

These provisional terms describe the acceptable use of the Roam pilot.

Eligibility and consent

Roam is intended exclusively for adults aged 18 or older. Starting a chat means you consent to the browser requesting access to camera and microphone, and to live audio/video being shared with your matched participant. Access can be withdrawn with the controls at any time.

Service limitations

Matching depends on how many compatible users are online. There are no promises of instant availability, a specific region, gender, or an uninterrupted connection. Regions and genders are optional or self-declared, not verified. Roam does not provide access to other platforms' private feeds.

Personal responsibility

Follow the Community rules and all applicable laws. Do not share others' content without their consent. Your partner may make their own recordings or screenshots beyond the app's control. Do not share sensitive identifying or financial information with strangers.

Operation

The service may end connections, reject abusive usage, or be unavailable for technical and safety reasons. Server and moderation controls may change as the application develops. For more detail on data handling, review Privacy.

Operator action required: before broad public launch, have an attorney review and finalize enforceable terms including the operating entity, legal jurisdiction, contact channel, consumer notices, and applicable dispute terms.
