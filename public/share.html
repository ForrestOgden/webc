Bring more people.
Make better connections.

Roam matches real adults who are online at the same time. When the queue is small, inviting others to join simultaneously creates more potential matches. No prerecorded videos, bots or guaranteed instant matches.

Invite your circle

Share the invitation with adult friends or group chats that welcome links. People who join enter the same global queue; invitations do not guarantee that two specific people will match.

Coordinate a live meetup

Choose a time, invite several adults, and ask everyone to join Roam together. Even a small group joining at once gives visitors a better chance of finding a live match.

Share as a creator

Link Roam from video descriptions, creator profiles, newsletters, or community posts when promotion is permitted. Explain that users can join random one-to-one video conversations with other adults online.

Add a link to your website

Copy this lightweight, outbound-link widget into your own website or community page. It doesn't access visitor cameras or embed an untrusted video frame.



Keep invitations voluntary. Don't mass-message strangers or promise someone a match when nobody is online. Roam is 18+ and visitors must follow the community rules.
