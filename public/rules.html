ROAM / RESPONSIBILITY

Community rules

Respect is a requirement. Anyone who uses Roam must follow these rules.

Adults only

You must be at least 18 years old to participate. If you believe a minor is on a call, end the connection and report it immediately. The current age checkbox is self-attestation, not identity verification.

Prohibited conduct

  • Sexual exposure or explicit material, sexual harassment, exploitation, grooming, or any abuse involving children.
  • Threats, intimidation, hateful or discriminatory attacks, bullying, or stalking.
  • Scams, impersonation, fraudulent solicitation, malware, or spam.
  • Displaying or sharing another person's private information without permission.
  • Recording or redistributing another participant's image or voice without their consent.

Your safety controls

Use Skip to leave any conversation at any time, Block to avoid a matching session, and Report for conduct that needs review. Do not disclose your address, passwords, financial details, or other identifying information to strangers.

Enforcement

Operators must review reports and take timely, proportionate action, including removals, bans and referral to appropriate authorities where required. A production operator needs reliable ban enforcement and human moderation; this pilot's session-only block function is not a comprehensive ban system.

Launch requirement: an actively monitored reporting queue, escalation plan for urgent child-safety reports, and verified age controls appropriate to the jurisdictions served.
