Privacy & data handling

How this prototype handles video, text, preferences, and safety reports.

Live video and audio

Roam requests camera and microphone access only after you press Start. Media is sent directly between matched browsers using WebRTC when possible, or via an optional TURN relay when configured. The Roam application server does not record or store calls. A partner may independently record or take screenshots without our knowledge.

Information handled

We process your chosen region, optional self-declared gender, connection status, session identifiers, signaling messages, and live text messages to provide matching and calls. Text messages are relayed to your current partner, but are not stored by this application's chat feature. Region and gender are not independently verified.

The server may process network IP addresses to limit abuse and handle network connections. The owner-only filter uses a hashed owner password and temporary protected session cookies; the actual password is not stored in plaintext by the application.

Reporting and moderation

When you send an in-app report, the application stores the report reason, optional details, and two random session identifiers in an operator-only report log. Reports are not published to other users. The operator must secure, review and delete those reports under an established retention policy.

Local data

Browsers store optional region and gender settings, plus an anonymous session identifier, using browser storage. You can clear this through your browser's site-data controls. A temporary owner session may be held in a protected cookie. The app does not use advertising trackers.

International use and deletion

Video connections may be made across borders and peer connections can reveal IP addresses to partners. A TURN relay can reduce this exposure, but may increase operating costs. Applicable privacy and children's-safety laws can impose requirements beyond this prototype.

Operator action required before wide public launch: supply a privacy contact, legal entity details, data-retention rules, regional compliance review, and an appropriate report-deletion workflow. This page explains current software behavior; it is not a substitute for a jurisdiction-specific privacy policy.
